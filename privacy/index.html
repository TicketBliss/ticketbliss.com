Privacy

Effective 2026-11-22. Operated by Ameya Holdings LLC.

TicketBliss is a one-page site that tells you we are coming soon. This page explains what we collect when you visit.

What is collected, and when

Nothing is collected until you choose Accept in the cookie banner. If you choose Decline or ignore the banner, no analytics run. After you accept, we use:

  • Google Analytics for usage statistics: which pages are visited, roughly where from, and on what kind of device.
  • Microsoft Clarity for session recordings and heatmaps that show how the page is used. Anything typed into a form field is masked before it leaves your browser.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Your choices

Your choice is stored in your browser. Use Cookie settings in the footer to change it at any time. Declining after accepting stops further collection.

Service providers

Google and Microsoft process this data for us. See the Google privacy statement and the Microsoft privacy statement.
